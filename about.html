About TripMind AI

TripMind AI is a travel-planning website focused on helping travelers understand the real cost and trade-offs behind a trip.

What we build

TripMind combines travel tools, destination guides, budget models, destination comparisons, and AI-assisted planning concepts.

Our approach

We aim to distinguish between estimates and live data, avoid unsupported price claims, and explain why a recommendation is made.
