05 · Trust

Frequently asked questions.

Are TripMind prices live?

Not by default. Planning estimates are labeled as estimates. Live provider prices should only be shown after a verified API, feed or partner deep-link is connected.

How does TripMind make money?

Potential revenue comes from contextual affiliate links and advertising. Affiliate relationships are disclosed and should not change factual estimates.

Can I change the affiliate provider?

Yes. Provider configuration is separated from the travel tools so a provider can be changed without rebuilding the interface.

Is the AI recommendation a guarantee?

No. It is a planning recommendation. Availability, prices, weather and personal preferences can change.
