08 · Trust

Frequently asked questions.

Are TripMind prices live?

Not by default. Planning estimates are clearly labeled. Live prices should only be shown after verified provider data or an API is connected.

How does TripMind make money?

Potential revenue comes from contextual affiliate links and advertising. Affiliate relationships are disclosed.

Can I change the affiliate provider?

Yes. The Affiliate Router is separated from the travel tools so providers can be changed without rebuilding the interface.

Does TripMind guarantee AdSense approval?

No. No website design can guarantee approval. The site should contain original useful content, clear navigation, transparent advertising, legal pages and compliant implementations.

Does TripMind use personal data?

The current planner performs its basic calculations in the browser and does not require an account. Any future analytics, AI APIs, affiliate tracking or advertising cookies should be documented and handled according to applicable requirements.
